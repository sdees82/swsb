# Appendix C: Plan Review Checklist
Alignment
The plan satisfies the approved specification.
The approach is grounded in repository evidence.
Existing patterns are reused or deviations are explained.
Alternatives and tradeoffs
Realistic alternatives were compared for meaningful decisions.
Complexity, security, privacy, accessibility, testability, cost, operations, and maintenance were considered.
Lasting decisions are captured appropriately.
Sequence
Work is divided into coherent, verifiable steps.
Risky assumptions and external interfaces are tested early.
Each step has a closing check.
Parallel tasks have real file, decision, and evidence boundaries.
Routing and control
The selected model or agent fits the task.
Your responsibilities and the worker, reviewer, and orchestrator roles are clear.
Permission boundaries match each role.
Retry, escalation, and stop conditions are defined.
Validation
Acceptance criteria map to tests or review.
Important tests are independent from implementation.
Manual, visual, security, accessibility, or operational checks are included where automation is insufficient.
