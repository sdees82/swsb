# Inspect the model

The model is `starter/public/models/championship-ring.glb`. The source project's `lib/rings/classic.ts` points to that URL; `AuthoredModel.tsx` loads it with `/draco/`. The registry ID is `classic`, and the definition export is `classicRing`. These technical identifiers and the asset filename stay unchanged.

## What the file contains

The GLB is 550,344 bytes and contains 14 meshes, 149,788 triangles and three embedded material slots. It requires `KHR_draco_mesh_compression`. It has no images, textures or external buffer/image URIs. Obtain and serve the local decoder from the installed dependency as SETUP.md describes; decoder code is not bundled.

SHA-256: `87870ccab314d1dfa1197d3def9a3a0aa9302037d2dd4db6c610d3cc8ea063ad`. Matching model version: `87870ccab314`.

| Mesh or group | Role | Later activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Ring_body` | Band and body | Ring metal; inner-band text is outside core scope |
| `Ring_lid` | Separable crown/lid | Top inspection and optional opening mechanism |
| `Player_name` | Player name panel | `playerName`, Player camera (`right-shoulder`) |
| `Player_caption` | Player caption panel | `position`, Player camera |
| `Player_crest` | Player crest panel | `playerNumber` rendered as a string; sport detail generated by the app |
| `Team_name` | Team name panel | Authored panel only; editable Team text excluded |
| `Team_caption` | Team caption panel | Authored panel only; editable Team text excluded |
| `Team_crest` | Team crest panel | Authored panel only; Team image/crest excluded |
| Each of the six panels' `_rim` meshes | Surrounding cast relief | Material-zone assignment; retain alongside the panel |

The embedded material names are `Body metal`, `Recessed gold` and `Cast metal`. In `lib/rings/classic.ts`, they map to `body`, `recess` and `cast`. Runtime `lib/materials.ts` and `lib/zoneMaterials.ts` build metal and stone materials, including procedural grain textures. Keep Ring metal, Lettering metal, Pavé and halo, and Side stones independent.

## What the app adds

The GLB is the authored metal. Editable inscriptions are geometry generated by `SurfaceText.tsx`, with the supplied collegiate and Anton font JSON. For this core, `Ring.tsx` adds Player lettering, the center face and top logo placement. Other inscriptions and Team customization in the larger source app are excluded. Its stone components generate stones. Lighting uses procedural light formers, so the archived HDRI is unnecessary.

The top and bottom inscription charts and center face are calculated from the crown profile. The center face is generated at runtime and has no authored host mesh. A plain GLB viewer therefore shows a simpler asset than the final app. Do not look for editable name strings or a finished logo texture inside the file.

`starter/model-metadata/ring-surfaces.json` contains sampled shoulder grids. Keep it with `crown-profile.json`, `ring-opening.json` and `ring-model-version.json`. The agent places these under `lib/` as setup describes. Its `classicSurfaces` declaration supplies the field-to-surface mapping.

Coordinates in the web model are Y up. The crown points toward +Y, the finger opening runs along Z, the player shoulder is at -X and the team shoulder is at +X. This is normalized application geometry, not a manufacturing measurement. Blender's import conversion uses its own coordinate convention; compare transformed coordinates before changing the charts.
